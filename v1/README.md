# Still — Distraction-Free Calm Space

> **A research-backed, distraction-free neuro-acoustic soundscape engine.**  
> Zero algorithms. Zero cognitive load. Instant peace for your mind and room.

---

## 🌿 Philosophy

Modern platforms like YouTube and streaming apps are designed to harvest attention with algorithms, feeds, and recommendations. When you simply want peaceful background audio to calm your nervous system or focus on work, you often get sucked into rabbit holes.

**Still** was built with a single, uncompromising rule:  
**Open the app $\rightarrow$ Instant calm $\rightarrow$ Zero distraction $\rightarrow$ Focus on life.**

---

## ✨ Features

- **Procedural Neuro-Acoustic Synthesizer**: Generates multi-layered ambient soundscapes in real-time using the Web Audio API. 100% offline-capable, zero buffering, and non-repeating continuous flow.
- **7 Research-Backed Soundscapes**:
  1. **Alpha Wave Sanctuary**: 432 Hz warm Rhodes/analog chords + 10 Hz binaural alpha beats for cortisol and stress reduction.
  2. **Deep Delta Sleep & Rest**: 2.5 Hz delta entrainment + sub-harmonic bass for melatonin stimulation and restorative sleep.
  3. **Brownian Rain Meditation**: Deep $1/f^2$ brown noise + spatial rainfall + distant thunder for amygdala quieting and acoustic noise masking.
  4. **Celestial Zen Garden**: Tibetan singing bowls + 528 Hz Solfeggio love tone + bamboo chimes.
  5. **Forest Dusk & Hearth**: Biophilic night crickets + fireplace embers + gentle wind.
  6. **Cosmic Float & Stillness**: Non-predictive zero-gravity ambient swells for cognitive prefrontal cortex rest.
  7. **Deep Flow & Study**: 6 Hz theta focus pulses + vinyl warmth for ADHD calming and deep flow states.
- **Generative Acoustic Piano**: Gentle Brian Eno-inspired pentatonic piano notes drifting in organically over ambient pad layers.
- **Resonant 0.1 Hz Breathing Core**: Central visualizer pulsing at 6 breaths per minute (4s Inhale, 2s Hold, 4s Exhale) to optimize Heart Rate Variability (HRV).
- **Auto-Ghost Inactivity Mode**: After 5 seconds of inactivity, all UI controls fade out smoothly, turning your screen into pure living room ambient art.
- **4 Calming Color Themes**:
  - 🌙 **Midnight Obsidian** (Default night mode)
  - ☀️ **Sunrise Amber** (Daylight warmth)
  - 🌲 **Forest Biophilic** (Stress reduction)
  - 🌅 **Lavender Dusk** (Twilight melatonin prep)
- **Essential Controls**: Volume slider, quick mute, sleep timer ($15\text{m} \to \infty$), and full-screen Zen mode.
- **100% Responsive**: Seamlessly scales from smartphones and tablets to desktop monitors.

## ⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `Space` | Play / Pause |
| `N` | Next Soundscape |
| `P` | Previous Soundscape |
| `M` | Mute / Unmute |
| `T` | Switch Color Theme |
| `F` | Toggle Fullscreen Zen |

## 🚀 Quick Start

No installations or dependencies required.

1. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.
2. Tap anywhere on the screen to enter the calm space.

Or serve locally:
```bash
python3 -m http.server 3456
```
Then visit `http://localhost:3456`.
